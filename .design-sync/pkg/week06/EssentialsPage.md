### EssentialsPage()

The whole Week 6 essentials page: topbar, the hero ("Seventeen essentials, one Marvel corpus"),
Part A (documents: weights, cosine, contrast, topics), Part B (words: contexts, PMI, vectors,
GloVe), the takeaways and the footer. It takes no props. Render it inside `<CorridorRoot page>`,
which keeps `main` free for the page's own layout.

```tsx
<CorridorRoot page>
  <EssentialsPage />
</CorridorRoot>
```
