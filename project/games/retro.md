Games that debuted in arcades (Pac-Man, Street Fighter II, Mortal Kombat, Tekken 3 and others) are left out even when they had home ports, because they belong on the arcade list. Numbered sequels that keep the same core loop are folded into the series' defining entry and named in the Game column; sequels the sources rank as separate landmarks (Super Mario 64, Ocarina of Time) keep their own row.
