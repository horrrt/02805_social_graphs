The Marvel network gets language

Headline question and the one figure that answers it. Pick the strongest of the seven sections once the results are in.

Opening

What the data is: 303 English Wikipedia pages from Category:Marvel Comics superheroes, frozen 26 August 2026, and the links between them. What a link means, what a token is, and which question the post answers. Self-contained: no earlier post needed.

Turn links into relationships

Who are the friends, foes and family in the Marvel network?

Course suggestion: For each link from A to B, the sentence on A's page that mentions B, labelled from a small word list (enemy, ally, married, teammate, killed). Do enemies sit in different communities? Script: analysis/week05_relations.py. Owner: not assigned yet.
