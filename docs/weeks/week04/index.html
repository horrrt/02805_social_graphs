Do the clusters follow official job groups?

SOC comparison

The government groups occupations by their first two SOC digits. We compare those labels with the clusters found from hiring patterns. The shuffled bars keep the clusters fixed and scramble only the official labels.

Cluster × official major group

Observed match versus shuffled labels

💡 What to notice The observed NMI is higher than the shuffled average when the network’s bundles resemble official occupational groups. It is still a comparison, not evidence that the government labels caused the hiring pattern.
How the comparison works

We keep certified H-1B filings, collapse employer spellings to company families, and count one shared employer per edge. The projection is clustered 100 times with different seeds; the page reports the best modularity run. NMI is calculated across all occupations and compared with 100 random permutations of the SOC major-group labels. FY2024 is kept as a stability check in the data file.
