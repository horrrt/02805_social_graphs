      What exactly did we measure? +

      Source: the course’s frozen Wikipedia category snapshot, dated 26 August 2026. There are 303 character articles and 1,784 directed links. A → B means that article A links to article B. Incoming and outgoing counts include only articles in this roster.

      The main component has 277 nodes when direction is ignored. The nine-node island has 22 internal arcs. Seventeen nodes have no links in either direction. These are properties of the snapshot, not claims about all Marvel characters, friendship, readership, or cultural popularity.

      The full node roster is loaded before the edges so the 17 isolates are retained. Every interactive count and connection is derived from those files.

      Character roster ↗ · Directed links ↗ · Download the presentation data ↓

      What do the distributions show? +

      Incoming links have a longer tail: the maximum is 106, versus 28 outgoing links. Across all 303 nodes, Pearson correlation between the two counts is 0.496. They are related, but describe different aspects of the network.

      Can we trust the snapshot? +

      The saved spot check, made for the original 2 September post, compared outgoing links for three articles with Wikipedia’s API: Betsy Braddock (28), Radian (6), and Baymax (0). All three matched the snapshot.

      This is a three-article check, not a validation of the entire dataset. Wikipedia may change after the snapshot; redirects and category boundaries can affect which links are included.
