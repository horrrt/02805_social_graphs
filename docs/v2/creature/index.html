    The takeaway

    Repair components, not just headcounts.

    Removing Spider-Man strands five separate single articles. Connecting all five back takes at least five new undirected links. Removing Black Widow strands three articles in two components, so two links suffice.

    A node can have many neighbours and still be replaceable for connectivity. Removing Hulk leaves all 276 remaining core articles together. The creature stays healthy even though a famous article has gone.

    What the data means

    303 article entries and 1,784 directed links from the course’s frozen 26 August 2026 snapshot. A link means one article links to another within this roster. It does not measure friendship, hero strength, popularity or the whole Marvel universe.

    Limits of the experiment

    This creature measures only connectivity, not all aspects of network robustness. All node removals and added links are counterfactual. A repair need not correspond to an editorially meaningful Wikipedia link. The 17 original isolates and nine-article island are outside this core-only scenario.

    Methods and reproducibility

    The browser deletes a selected article from the original 277-node undirected main component, computes every connected component, and treats the largest as the body. Repair edges are added to a separate in-memory scenario. The minimum number of added edges needed to reconnect k nonempty components is k − 1. The visual uses one dot per remaining article, with component membership determining placement.

    Analysis and source code on GitHub. All experiments run locally in your browser; the frozen data is never edited.
