The takeaway

The same snapshot can answer different questions.

Baymax cannot reach Spider-Man because Baymax has no links in this roster. Spider-Man leads incoming links. Removing Hulk leaves the 276 other core nodes connected. Rewiring preserves degree while changing the answers to path, triangle and removal questions.

The desktop contains working tools, not simulated terminal output. Free-play apps are marked as experiments, not posts. Their inputs, definitions and limitations travel with their results.

Evidence and details.

What the data means

303 article entries and 1,784 directed links from the course’s frozen 26 August 2026 snapshot. A link means one article links to another within this roster. It does not measure friendship, hero strength, popularity or the whole Marvel universe.

Limits of the experiment

The terminal is a graph command interpreter, not a shell. Its local rewiring state lasts until reset or reload. Directed paths always use original article links; undirected core experiments can use the current rewired scenario. Short descriptions are sufficient for a search demonstration but not a full-article NLP hand-in.

Methods and reproducibility

Browser commands share the same tested breadth-first search, connected-component, edge-swap and random-walk implementations as the cabinets. Interactive rewiring starts from the original 277-node core, rejects loops, duplicate edges and disconnected proposals, and accepts up to 2,000 swaps. It is a seeded demonstration, not the recorded 1,000-draw null ensemble or a claim of uniform sampling. TF-IDF uses log term frequency, smoothed inverse document frequency and cosine similarity over the 303 supplied descriptions.

Analysis and source code on GitHub. All experiments run locally in your browser; the frozen data is never edited.

AI use and authorship
