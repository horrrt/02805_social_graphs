About

We are a three-person group in 02805 Social Graphs and Interactions at DTU. The course hands every group the same frozen snapshot of Wikipedia's Category:Marvel Comics superheroes: 303 character articles, with an arc from A to B whenever A's article links to B's. Same data for everyone, so the only thing that varies is the question you ask of it.

Every post here shows its own working. The analysis lives in notebooks in the repository, and every number quoted on the site comes out of a script you can run yourself.
