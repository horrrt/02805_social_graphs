The Marvel network gets language

Does a character's place in the link network show in the words of its page? We read the 303 Marvel Wikipedia pages as text, with this week's tools: tokens, counts, n-grams, concordances and the document-term matrix, and asked seven questions where the words meet the links.

A link is an editor's choice to point to a page, not a friendship in the comics.
