What does each metro group add to modularity?

Each group holds slightly more filing weight than chance would give it, and the three surpluses add up to Q = 0.049.

The brief works modularity out by hand, one community at a time. Here is the same sum for section 1's three groups.

The formula

For each group c, the share of all link weight that falls inside it, minus the share a network with the same strengths would put there by chance.

Q = Σc [ wc / W − ( sc / 2W )² ]

💡What to notice Census regions score Q = 0.002, barely above one big group, while the page's groups score 0.049. Q says how grouped a split is. It cannot say whether the split is the right one.

Method

W is the total link weight of section 1's full network, 1,234,499: two metros are linked when a company files in both, weighted by the smaller of its two filing counts. A metro's strength is the sum of its link weights; sc adds them up over the group. The sum matches igraph's modularity to nine decimal places.

More numbers

1,000 shuffles of the three group labels, keeping the group sizes, average Q = -0.020 (sd 0.007). Putting every metro in one group gives exactly 0.
