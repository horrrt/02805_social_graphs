How many groups are there if you change what counts as a group?

Anything from one to forty. Below γ = 1 Louvain finds one group; at 1.25 it finds 11 and at 1.5 it finds 27, and those finer groups follow Census regions more closely.

The A dial on modularity: Qγ = Σc [ wc/W − γ (sc/2W)² ]. γ = 1 is standard modularity; a larger γ favours smaller groups. scales modularity's chance term. The brief uses it to fix the resolution limit: maximising Q merges groups with fewer than about √(2m) links inside.

💡What to notice At γ = 1 the finer groups agree with Census regions at NMI 0.14; at γ = 1.5 they reach 0.49, while their agreement with the page drops to 0.41. The resolution limit does not bite at γ = 1: in filing weight √(2W) is 1,571, and the smallest group holds 133,566 inside.

Method

igraph's community_multilevel with its resolution parameter and community_leiden with the modularity objective, 100 seeds each, on the full weighted network. √(2m) is the brief's rule of thumb for links; with filing weights we use √(2W) as the same yardstick.

Why Leiden changes nothing here

Leiden fixes Louvain's disconnected communities. Every metro links to every other in the full network, so no group can come apart, and Leiden returns the same two splits at the same modularity (best 0.057).
