Five years of filings

Certified filings fell 14% in FY2023, rose to 537,796 in FY2025, and fell 9.7% in FY2026 against the same months a year earlier. The share placed at a client fell every year from FY2023: 22.6%, 21.3%, 19.5%, and 18.1% in the first nine months of FY2026.

!

How to read the years. FY runs October to September. FY2026 covers October 2025 to June 2026 and is drawn hollow. October 2025, the month of the federal shutdown, holds 1,306 certified filings against 35,258 a year earlier, so compare FY2026 with earlier years on matching months.
