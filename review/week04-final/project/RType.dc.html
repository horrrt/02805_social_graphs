Type scale: one set of sizes for the whole site

Built from the sizes Week 4 and the home page already use. Every page, every chart and every canvas board takes its type from these ten tokens and three families. Charts draw at their real width, so a label set at 12.5 is 12.5 on screen.

Gone: Arial in buttons and inputs, Barlow as a body face on Play, and the extra stacks in chart code. The styleguide's opt-in skins (editorial serif, poster) keep their own families but use these sizes.

Rules

  1. 1
    Only tokens.No font-size in px, rem or em outside the token file; chart code reads the same tokens.
  2. 2
    Charts draw 1:1.An SVG chart takes its box's width, it is never scaled down; text stays at its token size.
  3. 3
    Nothing below 11.5.Not in charts, tables, legends or footers.
  4. 4
    Weight makes hierarchy inside a size.A chart title is body at 700, an eyebrow is caption at 700; no new size for emphasis.
  5. 5
    Play keeps one jumbo size.--fs-jumbo 96, for the arcade scoreboard only.
