The section rail: how it behaves

The dots down the left margin of every section board: one per section, the open one larger, with its questions as small dots under it. Play with it on the left: each click stands in for scrolling there. The four states it moves through are in the middle, the rules on the right. Hover anywhere on a rail to see every name at once. Dot sizes, spacing, colour, how the open dot looks, labels and the trigger line are tweaks.

The rules

  1. 1Follows the scrollThe current item is the last section or question whose top has passed the trigger line, {{triggerPct}}% down the window.
  2. 2One section openOnly the current section opens: its dot grows and its questions appear as small dots under it. Every other section is one small hollow dot.
  3. 3Two levels of currentThe open section is a large accent dot with a soft halo, or a ring if the tweak says so; the current question fills with ink.
  4. 4Names on hoverHovering anywhere on the rail, or focusing it with the keyboard, shows every name at once; the item under the pointer is dark. The labels line up in one column and lie over the page's left edge until the pointer leaves. The names carry the numbers, so the dots need none. A tweak switches back to one name at a time.
  5. 5Click to jumpA click scrolls to that section; a deep-dive item also opens its box.
  6. 6Wide screens onlyThe rail hides below a 1,240 px window, where the column has no margin for it.
